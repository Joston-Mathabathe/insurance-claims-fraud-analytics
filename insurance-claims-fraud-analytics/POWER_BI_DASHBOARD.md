- Total Claims
- Total Claim Value

- Review Flag Rate

Charts:
- Claims by Policy Type

## Page 2: Investigation Prioritisation
Show:
- Claim amount
- Claim-to-premium ratio

- Documents submitted

Add slicers for policy type and claim amount band.

## Page 3: Model Performance
Show:
- Accuracy
- Precision

- F1
- ROC-AUC

Include a short explanation of why precision and recall matter operationally.
