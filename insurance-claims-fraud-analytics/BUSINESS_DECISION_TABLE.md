# Business Decision Table

| Analytical output | Business question | Possible response |
|---|---|---|
| High review rate in a policy type | Is this segment generating disproportionate investigation workload? | Investigate the underlying claim patterns |
| High claim-to-premium ratio | Is the potential financial exposure material? | Prioritise appropriate risk review |
| Low precision | Are too many claims being flagged unnecessarily? | Review threshold and operational impact |
| Low recall | Could many target cases be missed? | Review threshold and model features |
| High investigation volume | Can the claims team handle the queue? | Consider operational capacity |
| Model performance changes | Has the underlying claim environment changed? | Monitor and investigate model performance |

The table supports decision-making without automatically declaring a claim fraudulent.
