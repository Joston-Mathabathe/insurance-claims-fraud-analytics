# Model Card

## Intended Use
Portfolio demonstration of classification for prioritising insurance claims for additional review.

## Not Intended For
Automatic fraud declarations, automatic claim rejection, real customer decisions, or production deployment.

## Data
6,000 synthetic claim records generated for educational purposes.

## Models
- Logistic Regression
- Decision Tree
- Random Forest

## Metrics
Accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, ROC-AUC and average precision.

## Limitations
Synthetic data cannot represent the true distribution of insurance claims or fraud. Real deployment would require representative labelled outcomes, validation, fairness testing, governance, monitoring and regulatory review.
